Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sprintiverse collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you use the Sprintiverse website and software-as-a-service platform (the "Service"). We aim to process personal data lawfully, fairly, transparently, and only for legitimate purposes connected with the Service.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Sprintiverse, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, authentication identifiers, and profile information.
  • Workspace information: workspace name, settings, memberships, roles, folders, rules, and configuration.
  • Operational data: orders, customer information, messages, attachments, automation data, and other information you or your authorized users submit to the Service.
  • Payment and subscription information: plan, subscription status, transaction identifiers, billing cycle, and related records. Payment credentials are processed by our payment provider and are not intended to be stored by Sprintiverse.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser/device information, logs, timestamps, security events, and information about how the Service is used.

2. Google Authentication

If you choose Google authentication, Google provides Sprintiverse with information needed to create or authenticate your account, such as your email address, name, provider identifier, and profile information made available through the authorization flow. We use this information to authenticate you and maintain your Sprintiverse account. Your use of Google is also subject to Google's policies.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and operate Sprintiverse; authenticate users; create and maintain workspaces; process subscriptions and payments; run automations and integrations requested by you; provide support; communicate service-related notices; prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; troubleshoot and improve reliability; comply with legal obligations; and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Customer Data and Business Information

When you connect an integration or upload operational data, you remain responsible for ensuring that you have the lawful authority to provide and process that information. Sprintiverse processes Customer Data to provide the features you request and does not treat your customer/order data as a product to sell to advertisers.

5. Service Providers and Third Parties

We may use trusted service providers to operate Sprintiverse, including infrastructure, authentication, analytics, communications, security, and payment providers. For example, Supabase may provide authentication and database infrastructure, and Razorpay may process payments. These providers process information according to their roles, contracts, and applicable policies and laws.

6. Payments

When you purchase a paid Sprintiverse plan, payment information is handled through the payment provider presented at checkout. Razorpay's own privacy and payment terms apply to information it processes. Sprintiverse receives and stores subscription and transaction records needed to manage your account and billing.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Sprintiverse may use cookies, local storage, session technologies, and similar mechanisms required for authentication, security, preferences, and service functionality. We may also use limited technical analytics to understand reliability and usage. You can control cookies through your browser, although disabling essential technologies may affect functionality.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security, satisfy contractual and legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain appropriate business records. Retention periods can vary by the type of information and the purpose for which it is processed.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information we process. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so you should also protect your credentials and notify us of suspected unauthorized access.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, restriction or objection to certain processing, and grievance handling. Requests may be made through the contact or support channel provided by Sprintiverse. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

11. Indian Data Protection Law

Where applicable, Sprintiverse will process personal data in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules and requirements. 12. International Processing

Because Sprintiverse may use cloud and third-party infrastructure, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you are located. Where required, we will use appropriate legal and contractual measures for such processing.

13. Children's Privacy

Sprintiverse is intended for businesses and professional users and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly design the Service to collect personal data from children in violation of applicable law.

14. Data Deletion

You may request closure of your account and deletion of personal data, subject to information we are required or permitted to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or legitimate business purposes.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Sprintiverse changes or legal requirements evolve. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

16. Contact and Grievances

For privacy questions, data-rights requests, or complaints, use the contact or support channel made available through Sprintiverse. We will review and respond to privacy requests in accordance with applicable law.
