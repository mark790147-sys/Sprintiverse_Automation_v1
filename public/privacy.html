Sprintiverse

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026 · Consent version: 2026-08-31

This Privacy Policy is intended to explain Sprintiverse's personal-data practices in plain language. It is a technical and operational privacy notice and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before launch.

1. Who We Are

Sprintiverse is a software-as-a-service platform for order management, workflow rules, and automation. Where Sprintiverse determines why and how personal data is processed, Sprintiverse acts as the Data Fiduciary for that processing.

2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, authentication identifiers and profile information.
  • Workspace data: workspace name, memberships, roles, folders, rules and configuration.
  • Operational data: orders, customer names and emails, automation records and other business data submitted through connected services.
  • Subscription data: plan, subscription status, transaction identifiers and billing records. Full payment-card credentials are not intended to be stored by Sprintiverse.
  • Security/technical data: IP address, request metadata, timestamps, browser/device information and security events.
  • Consent and privacy-request records used to demonstrate and administer privacy choices.

3. Purposes and Consent

We collect personal data only for a stated purpose. Account creation and service operation use a separate account-processing consent. Optional marketing and optional product analytics are separate choices and are not bundled into the account consent.

Consent is requested in clear language before the signup transaction. There are no pre-ticked optional consent boxes. You can withdraw optional marketing or analytics consent through Privacy & Data Rights. Withdrawal is designed to be as easy as giving consent.

Where the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies, Sprintiverse does not rely on a private-company "legitimate interest" basis as a substitute for consent. Other processing may occur where a specific lawful basis or legal obligation applies.

4. Google Authentication

If you choose Google sign-in, Sprintiverse requests the minimum identity information needed for authentication. Depending on the OAuth response, this can include your name, email address, provider identifier and profile information. Google-provided data is treated as personal data under the same privacy controls as directly collected account data. Google OAuth is not used to request unrelated account permissions.

5. How We Use Data

We use data to create and secure accounts; operate workspaces; provide order management and automation; process subscriptions; perform integrations requested by the user; provide support; prevent fraud and abuse; investigate security incidents; send service notices; honor privacy choices; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Data Minimization and Retention

We aim to collect only data needed for the stated purpose. Account information is retained while the account is active and through the applicable deletion process. Operational order data is retained while needed for the workspace's order-management purpose and is then deleted or anonymized where feasible. Security/log data containing personal information is targeted for a maximum 90-day retention period unless needed for an active incident or legal obligation. Consent and privacy-request records may be retained for the period reasonably required to demonstrate compliance.

7. Third-Party Processors and International Transfers

Sprintiverse uses third-party processors to operate the Service. Current processor categories include Supabase for database/authentication infrastructure, Vercel for hosting/CDN, Google for OAuth authentication, and Razorpay for payments. Some processors may process data outside India, including in the United States. The Privacy & Data Rights center publishes the current processor inventory, recipient country, data categories and DPA status.

We maintain a processor inventory and review vendor/sub-processor arrangements. Where a Data Processing Agreement is required, Sprintiverse is responsible for obtaining and maintaining the appropriate contractual arrangement.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information processed, including access controls, database row-level security, authentication controls, rate limiting where implemented, and incident response procedures. We do not store raw authentication OTP values in application data.

9. Your DPDP Rights

Subject to applicable law, you can request information about personal data held by Sprintiverse, correction of inaccurate account data, erasure of personal data, withdrawal of consent, and exercise of the right to nominate another individual for rights requests in the event of death or incapacity.

The Privacy & Data Rights center provides self-service controls for access requests, correction, optional consent withdrawal, nominee registration, account deletion requests, and grievances.

10. Account Deletion

A deletion request initiates the Sprintiverse deletion workflow. Associated account/workspace personal data is intended to be removed from the primary application systems. Data that must be retained for legal, financial, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or other applicable obligations may be retained for the required period and isolated where feasible.

11. Children's Data

Sprintiverse is a business/professional SaaS product and is not intended for users under 18. We do not intentionally target children or knowingly collect children's data for behavioral tracking or targeted advertising. The service operator must maintain an effective age-enforcement and, where applicable, verifiable parental-consent mechanism before accepting under-18 users; the current product should therefore not be marketed or opened to under-18 users until that mechanism is implemented.

12. Grievances

Privacy grievances can be submitted through the Privacy & Data Rights center and are logged for acknowledgement and resolution tracking. The service operator must designate a named Grievance Officer and publish that person's direct contact details before launch. This policy currently identifies the role but intentionally does not invent a person's name or contact details.

13. Data Breach Response

Sprintiverse maintains a breach-response workflow covering detection, internal escalation, incident documentation, affected-data analysis, remediation, and notification planning. Where required, notifications to the Data Protection Board and affected Data Principals will be made according to applicable law and current regulatory instructions.

14. Changes to Consent and This Policy

When a material privacy or consent change requires renewed consent, existing users will be prompted to make a new choice. The active consent version is displayed in the privacy controls. Updated policy versions are published on this page with a new effective date.

15. Contact

Use the self-service privacy center for data-rights requests and grievances: Privacy & Data Rights.
